<h3>📢 招生信息</h3>

<p>
我们长期招收具有<strong>自我驱动力和研究热情</strong>的优秀学生，
包括<strong>科研实习生、硕士生和博士生</strong>。
如果你对<strong>自然语言处理、大语言模型以及智能体</strong>
等前沿方向感兴趣，并希望参与具有影响力的研究工作，欢迎联系加入我们。
</p>

<h5 class="mt-4 mb-3 text-primary fw-bold">🌱 科研实习生</h5>

<p>
    面向<strong>上海交通大学或其他高校学生</strong>，
    希望能够稳定投入时间参与研究，以发表高水平论文为目标。线上线下灵活安排。
    我们会提供<strong>系统化、细致的科研指导</strong>，包括选题、方法实验设计与论文写作等全过程指导，通过<strong>定期1对1交流</strong>，帮助同学逐步成长为能够独立开展研究的科研人员。
</p>

<h5 class="mt-4 mb-3 text-primary fw-bold">🎓 硕士 / 博士研究生</h5>

<p>
欢迎具有<strong>扎实基础和科研潜力</strong>的同学报考或申请加入课题组。
学生将深度参与前沿科研项目，
在导师指导下开展研究并发表成果，
鼓励在<strong>ACL、EMNLP、NeurIPS、ICML等顶级会议</strong>发表论文。

对于希望进入工业界的同学，可推荐至<strong>国内外头部科技公司</strong>实习或工作；
对于希望继续深造的同学，可<strong>推荐至国内外高校攻读博士</strong>。
</p>

<h3>📢 Open Positions</h3>

<p>
Our group is always looking for highly <strong>self-motivated and passionate</strong> students,
including <strong>undergraduate interns, Master's students, and PhD students</strong>.
If you are interested in cutting-edge research topics such as 
<strong>Natural Language Processing, Large Language Models, and AI Agents</strong>,
and would like to work on impactful research problems,
you are very welcome to join our group.
</p>

<hr>

<h5 class="mt-4 mb-3 text-primary fw-bold">🚀 Research Areas (including but not limited to)</h5>

<ul>
    <li class="mb-2"><strong>Agent Learning and Reasoning</strong>: training LLM agents, planning and reasoning, multi-agent collaboration</li>
    <li class="mb-2"><strong>Mechanistic Interpretability</strong>: understanding the internal mechanisms of foundation models and methods for model control and intervention</li>
    <li class="mb-2"><strong>Trustworthiness and Factualness</strong>: trustworthy AI, factual knowledge, bias mitigation</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h5 class="mt-4 mb-3 text-primary fw-bold">
🌱 Student Mentorship
</h5>

<ul>
    <li class="mb-2">
        <strong>Open and supportive research environment</strong><br>
        Our group does not enforce strict attendance requirements.
        Instead, we emphasize students' research interests and long-term development.
        We encourage students to propose their own ideas and participate in the entire research process,
        including problem formulation, method design, implementation, and paper writing.
        Based on each student's strengths, research interests, and future goals,
        I will help design an appropriate research plan and development path.
        We also have regular <strong>one-on-one meetings</strong> for discussion and guidance,
        especially providing detailed research mentoring for students who are new to research.
    </li>
    <li class="mb-2">
        <strong>Strong computing resources</strong><br>
        Our lab provides multiple GPU servers (e.g., RTX 4090, A6000, A800),
        along with a well-equipped research environment and office facilities.
    </li>
    <li class="mb-2">
        <strong>High-impact research outcomes</strong><br>
        Students are encouraged to publish high-quality and impactful papers
        at top conferences such as ACL, EMNLP, NeurIPS, and ICML.
        We also support open-source projects and patent applications.
    </li>
    <li class="mb-2">
        <strong>Career development and recommendation</strong><br>
        For students interested in industry careers,
        we can recommend internships or full-time positions at leading tech companies.
        For students planning to pursue further academic study,
        we can provide recommendation letters and support applications to top universities for graduate or doctoral programs.
    </li>
</ul>


<hr>

<h5 class="mt-4 mb-3 text-primary fw-bold">
🔍 What We Expect From You
</h5>

<ul>
    <li class="mb-2">
        <strong>Clear goals</strong>: understand your research interests and are willing to invest time and effort in exploring them.
    </li>
    <li class="mb-2">
        <strong>Self-motivation and curiosity</strong>: proactive learning, curiosity about new ideas, and willingness to explore challenging problems.
    </li>
    <li class="mb-2">
        <strong>Strong foundation</strong>: solid background in mathematics (e.g., probability, linear algebra) and programming.
    </li>
    <li class="mb-2">
        <strong>Commitment</strong>: commit a stable amount of time and participate in research over a sustained period.
    </li>
</ul>
