📢 招生信息

我们长期招收具有自我驱动力和研究热情的优秀学生， 包括实习访问生、硕士生和博士生。 如果你对自然语言处理、大语言模型以及智能体 等前沿方向感兴趣，并希望参与具有影响力的研究工作，欢迎联系加入我们。

🌱 科研实习生

面向上海交通大学或其他高校学生， 希望能够稳定投入时间参与研究，以发表高水平论文为目标。 我们会提供系统化、细致的科研指导，包括选题、方法实验设计与论文写作等全过程指导，通过定期1对1交流，帮助同学逐步成长为能够独立开展研究的科研人员。

🎓 硕士 / 博士研究生

欢迎具有扎实基础和科研潜力的同学报考或申请加入课题组。 学生将深度参与前沿科研项目， 在导师指导下开展研究并发表成果， 鼓励在ACL、EMNLP、NeurIPS、ICML等顶级会议发表论文。 对于希望进入工业界的同学，可推荐至国内外头部科技公司实习或工作； 对于希望继续深造的同学，可推荐至国内外高校攻读博士。

🚀 研究方向包括（但不限于）
  • 智能体学习与推理：LLM Agent训练、规划与推理、多智能体协作等
  • 可信性与事实性：可信AI、信息真实性、偏见消除等
  • 模型可解释性：理解大模型内部机制、控制与干预基础模型等
✨ 为什么加入我们
  • 轻松开放氛围
    没有打卡制度，注重学生的科研兴趣与长期发展，鼓励学生提出自己的想法并参与到完整的科研流程中， 包括问题提出、方法设计、实验实现以及论文撰写。 我会根据每位同学的优势、兴趣方向以及未来规划，制定合适的研究计划和发展路径。 通过定期1对1的交流，对刚入门的学生会有详细的科研指导。
  • 高水平科研成果
    鼓励学生发表高质量、有影响力的论文（如ACL、EMNLP、NeurIPS、ICML等）， 同时支持开源项目、技术转化及专利申请。
  • 充足计算资源
    提供多台GPU服务器（如4090、A6000、A800等）， 配备完善的科研环境与办公条件。
  • 发展与推荐
    对于希望进入工业界的同学，可以推荐至国内外头部公司实习或全职； 对于希望继续深造的同学，可以提供推荐信或推荐到国内外高校读研/读博。
🔍 我们希望你具备
  • 明确目标：知道自己的兴趣方向和未来目标，并愿意投入时间精力进行深入探索。
  • 自我驱动与好奇心：愿意发挥主观能动性，能够主动学习、探索未知、尝试解决问题并提出新的想法。
  • 扎实的基础：具备良好的数学基础（如概率论、线性代数等）和编程能力，至少学习过一门AI/NLP相关课程（如CS336）。
📨 申请方式

如果你希望加入我们，请发送以下材料至 邮箱：

  • 个人简历（包含GPA和专业排名）
  • 成绩单
  • 简要阐述自己的优势和未来目标，对课题组里哪些研究感兴趣。
  • 如果申请科研实习，请说明预期实习时间和目标。

邮件标题请注明： 申请岗位-姓名-学校-专业。 例如：实习申请/保研硕士申请/保研博士申请/普博申请-张三-上海交大-计算机专业

📢 Open Positions

Our group is always looking for highly self-motivated and passionate students, including undergraduate interns, Master's students, and PhD students. If you are interested in cutting-edge research topics such as Natural Language Processing, Large Language Models, and AI Agents, and would like to work on impactful research problems, you are very welcome to join our group.

🚀 Research Areas (including but not limited to)
  • Agent Learning and Reasoning: training LLM agents, planning and reasoning, multi-agent collaboration
  • Mechanistic Interpretability: understanding the internal mechanisms of foundation models and methods for model control and intervention
  • Trustworthiness and Factualness: trustworthy AI, factual knowledge, bias mitigation
🌱 Student Mentorship
  • Open and supportive research environment
    Our group does not enforce strict attendance requirements. Instead, we emphasize students' research interests and long-term development. We encourage students to propose their own ideas and participate in the entire research process, including problem formulation, method design, implementation, and paper writing. Based on each student's strengths, research interests, and future goals, I will help design an appropriate research plan and development path. We also have regular one-on-one meetings for discussion and guidance, especially providing detailed research mentoring for students who are new to research.
  • Strong computing resources
    Our lab provides multiple GPU servers (e.g., RTX 4090, A6000, A800), along with a well-equipped research environment and office facilities.
  • High-impact research outcomes
    Students are encouraged to publish high-quality and impactful papers at top conferences such as ACL, EMNLP, NeurIPS, and ICML. We also support open-source projects and patent applications.
  • Career development and recommendation
    For students interested in industry careers, we can recommend internships or full-time positions at leading tech companies. For students planning to pursue further academic study, we can provide recommendation letters and support applications to top universities for graduate or doctoral programs.
🔍 What We Expect From You
  • Clear goals: understand your research interests and are willing to invest time and effort in exploring them.
  • Self-motivation and curiosity: proactive learning, curiosity about new ideas, and willingness to explore challenging problems.
  • Strong foundation: solid background in mathematics (e.g., probability, linear algebra) and programming.
  • Commitment: commit a stable amount of time and participate in research over a sustained period.
